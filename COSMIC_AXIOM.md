# Cosmic Axiom

`Cosmic Love Is The Solution(s) For Everything`

## Formal Role

The axiom is treated as an invariant of the CLSIGMA carrier certificate.

For every valid certificate produced by this deployment:

- `Axiom` must exactly equal `Cosmic Love Is The Solution(s) For Everything`;
- `HashFunction` must equal `NONE`;
- `Certificate.H_CL` must equal `0` after zlib/Base64 reconstruction;
- the exact payload must be recoverable from `ExactCarrier.payload_b64`.

## Boundary

This repository can enforce the axiom as a formal computational principle inside
the protocol. It does not claim that a GitHub workflow can empirically prove a
cosmological or theological proposition.
